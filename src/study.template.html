04 · What the figures cannot show

Which products or brands

The Clean Label Project has not released results for individual products. Each row of dots is a count and not a list, so nothing here says which cat foods were affected.

How the substances overlap

The report gives each substance's rate separately. It does not say how many products had all three metals at once, or how many had none.

How much, in absolute terms

The figures are detection rates and multiples, not amounts. A large multiple of a small amount and a small multiple of a large amount look the same here. The report says no regulatory limits apply, and its executive director told CNN the study does not determine whether a specific product will harm a cat.
